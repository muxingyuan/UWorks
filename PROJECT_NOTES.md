# UWorks project notes

## Future phase only: interactive voice consultation

The hero section's “Start a conversation” button currently scrolls to the Contact section.

In a future phase, consider changing this button to open an interactive voice conversation. A visitor could speak about their needs for one or two minutes, then receive a concise summary and suggested next steps.

This feature is intentionally not part of the current website build. Before implementation, define the visitor consent experience, privacy and retention policy, conversation limits, summary delivery method, accessibility fallback, and operating costs.
